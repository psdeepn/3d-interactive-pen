// hcp objection guide

Common Questions,
Clear Answers

A built-in objection handling system helps reps address HCP concerns in the field — all backed by clinical study data.

What makes Pen® different from other pens?
+
Pen® is reusable (up to 2 years), using Penfill® 3ml cartridges instead of the whole pen being discarded. It retains other pen's non-extending dose button and adds a higher 80-unit maximum dose. The environmental footprint is dramatically reduced.
Do patients need retraining to switch?
+
No significant retraining required. The familiar pen features are preserved, and learning to use Pen® takes under 10 minutes on average for pen-injector experienced patients.
Which insulins work with Pen®?
+
All insulins in Penfill® 3ml cartridges.
Does it have connectivity features?
+
No. Pen® does not offer digital connectivity. It is a focused, reliable mechanical device optimised for ease of use and sustainability.
What needles are compatible?
+
Other single-use disposable needles up to 8mm length (supplied separately).
